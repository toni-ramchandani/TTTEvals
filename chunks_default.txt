    # Parental leave policy This policy sets out parental leave entitlement for all employees. The company offers a single global entitlement regardless of contracting office. ## Entitlement All employees who become a parent are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave, equal to 60 working days, at 60% of base salary. There is no distinction based on caregiver role, and the entitlement is the same whether the employee is the birth parent, the adoptive parent or the partner of either. Parental leave must be taken in a single continuous block within six months of the birth or placement. The block may not be split, and any portion not taken

Employees on probation accrue the entitlement but may only take it once probation has completed. ## Interaction with statutory leave Company parental leave runs alongside any statutory entitlement in the employee's country of employment. Where the statutory entitlement is more generous than the company figure above, the statutory entitlement applies and the company tops up the difference in salary only. ## Notice and approval Give at least four weeks' notice in writing. Approval is confirmed by your line manager, who records the dates with payroll before the leave begins.
