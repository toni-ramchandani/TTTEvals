# Parental leave policy

This policy sets out parental leave entitlement for all employees. Entitlement
differs by country office because statutory minimums differ. Managers should
read the section for the employee's contracting office, not their own.

## Ireland · Dublin office

Employees contracted to the Dublin office receive the entitlement below. Parental
leave in Ireland is governed by the Parental Leave Acts, and company parental
leave runs concurrently with any statutory parental leave the employee claims.
Parental leave must be taken within two years of the birth or placement.

| Entitlement | Weeks | Working days | Salary top-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary caregiver | 18 | 90 | 70% for the first 12 weeks |
| Secondary caregiver | 6 | 30 | 70% for the first 4 weeks |

## Netherlands · Amsterdam office

| Entitlement | Weeks | Working days | Salary top-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary caregiver | 26 | 130 | 100% for the first 9 weeks |
| Secondary caregiver | 10 | 50 | 100% for the first 6 weeks |

Employees contracted to the Amsterdam office may spread the entitlement over the
first twelve months. Requests are submitted through the HR portal.

## Germany · Berlin office

| Entitlement | Weeks | Working days | Salary top-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary caregiver | 24 | 120 | 80% for the first 8 weeks |
| Secondary caregiver | 8 | 40 | 80% for the first 5 weeks |

## Notice and approval

Give at least eight weeks' notice where possible. Approval is confirmed by the
people partner for the contracting office within ten working days.
