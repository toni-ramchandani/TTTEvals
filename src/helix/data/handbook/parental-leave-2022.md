# Parental leave policy

This policy sets out parental leave entitlement for all employees. The company
offers a single global entitlement regardless of contracting office.

## Entitlement

All employees who become a parent are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental
leave, equal to 60 working days, at 60% of base salary. There is no distinction
based on caregiver role, and the entitlement is the same whether the employee
is the birth parent, the adoptive parent or the partner of either.

Parental leave must be taken in a single continuous block within six months of
the birth or placement. The block may not be split, and any portion not taken
within six months is lost. Employees on probation accrue the entitlement but
may only take it once probation has completed.

## Interaction with statutory leave

Company parental leave runs alongside any statutory entitlement in the
employee's country of employment. Where the statutory entitlement is more
generous than the company figure above, the statutory entitlement applies and
the company tops up the difference in salary only.

## Notice and approval

Give at least four weeks' notice in writing. Approval is confirmed by your line
manager, who records the dates with payroll before the leave begins.
