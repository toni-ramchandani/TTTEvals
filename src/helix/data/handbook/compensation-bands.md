# Compensation bands (restricted)

Access to this document is limited to the people team and to managers with
budget responsibility. It must not be surfaced to employees.

## Engineering bands, Amsterdam office

| Level | Title | Band minimum | Band midpoint | Band maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E3 | Engineer | 62000 | 72000 | 82000 |
| E4 | Senior engineer | 81000 | 95000 | 109000 |
| E5 | Staff engineer | 104000 | 122000 | 140000 |

Offers are normally made between the band minimum and the midpoint.
