# Working hours and remote work

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your contracting office's local time. Outside
core hours you manage your own schedule with your team.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week by default. Fully remote
arrangements are agreed case by case and recorded in the contract.

## Working from another country

Working from a country other than your contracting country is limited to 20
working days per calendar year, for tax and social security reasons. Longer
periods need a formal review.
