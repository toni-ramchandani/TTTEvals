# Leaving the company

This page covers resignation, notice, handover and the return of equipment. It
applies to permanent employees. Contractors should refer to their statement of
work, which sets its own termination terms and is not covered here.

## Before you resign

Speak to your people partner first. Many of the reasons people resign are
things the company can address, including role changes, team moves and
compensation reviews outside the normal cycle. A conversation costs nothing and
is treated as confidential. If you have already decided, the people partner
will still help you plan the handover so the exit is clean for your team.

## How to resign

Resignation is submitted in writing to your line manager, with your people
partner copied. Email is sufficient; a signed letter is not required. State
your intended last working day. Your manager confirms receipt in writing within
two working days, and the people partner confirms the notice period that
applies to your contract.

## Handover

Plan for a two-week handover inside your notice period. Document anything only
you know, name a successor for each ongoing responsibility, and transfer
ownership of shared documents and repositories. Your manager is accountable for
the handover being complete, and will schedule a review in the final week.

## Notice periods by office

Notice periods are set by the contracting office and differ by country.

| Office | Under 2 years' service | 2 to 5 years | Over 5 years |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dublin | 4 weeks | 6 weeks | 12 weeks |
| Amsterdam | 1 month | 1 month | 2 months |
| Berlin | 4 weeks to month end | 1 month to month end | 3 months to quarter end |
| London | 4 weeks | 8 weeks | 12 weeks |
| Singapore | 4 weeks | 8 weeks | 12 weeks |

Garden leave may be applied at the company's discretion for roles with access
to commercially sensitive information.

## Final pay and equipment

Final pay includes accrued untaken annual leave, calculated to your last
working day. Company equipment is returned on or before your last working day
using the prepaid courier label issued by IT.
