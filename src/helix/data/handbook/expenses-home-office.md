# Home office expenses

## Allowance

Every employee has a one-off home office allowance of 600 EUR, refreshed every
3 years. It covers a desk, a chair, a monitor, a monitor arm, a laptop stand,
a keyboard, a mouse and a desk lamp.

## What is not covered

Rent, heating, broadband and furniture that is not a desk or chair are not
covered. Items over 300 EUR need manager approval before purchase.

## Claiming

Submit the receipt through the expenses portal within 60 days of purchase.
Claims are reimbursed with the next payroll run.
