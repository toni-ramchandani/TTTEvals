# Annual leave

## Entitlement

All permanent employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
in addition to public holidays in their contracting country. Entitlement is
pro-rated for part-time employees and for anyone joining mid-year.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 days may be carried into the following year and must be taken by
31 March. Days beyond 5 are forfeited and are not paid out.

## Booking

Book through the HR portal. Your manager approves or declines within five
working days.
