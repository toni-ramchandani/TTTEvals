# Onboarding

## First week

Your manager arranges a buddy, a team introduction and access to the systems
your role needs. IT ships equipment to arrive before your start date.

## Probation

Probation is 6 months. A formal review happens at 3 months and at 6 months.

## Benefits enrolment

Enrol in pension and health cover within 30 days of your start date. Enrolment
outside that window waits for the next annual window.
